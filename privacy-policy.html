Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 9, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 9, 2026

Noise Evidence Recorder ("the App") is designed to measure and record ambient noise levels. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data stays on your device.

The App processes the following data locally on your device:

  • Audio data: Microphone input is analyzed in real-time to calculate decibel (dB) levels. Raw audio is never recorded or stored — only numerical dB values are saved.
  • Recording sessions: Session metadata (title, start/end time, average/peak dB values, user notes) is stored in a local database on your device.
  • Device information: Device model and Android version are included in generated PDF reports for context. This information is never transmitted.

2. Microphone Usage

The App requires microphone access to measure ambient noise levels. The microphone is used exclusively for decibel calculation. The App does not:

  • Record speech or conversations
  • Perform speech recognition
  • Transmit audio data to any server
  • Share audio data with third parties

Important: Microphone access is active only during measurement or recording sessions. A visible notification is displayed whenever background recording is active.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted database. Data includes:

  • Decibel readings (numerical values only)
  • Recording session information
  • User-entered notes

You can delete any recording session at any time from within the App. Deleting a session permanently removes all associated data.

4. Data Sharing

The App does not share data with any third party. When you use the "Share" or "PDF Export" feature, you control who receives the report — the App itself does not transmit data.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may include third-party advertising SDKs in the free version. These services may collect anonymized device identifiers for ad delivery purposes. You can remove ads by upgrading to Premium.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Access: View all your data within the App
  • Delete: Remove individual sessions or uninstall the App to delete all data
  • Revoke permissions: Disable microphone access at any time in device settings

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
